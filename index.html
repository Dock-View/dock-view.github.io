DockView

Window previews for the macOS Dock.

Hover over any app icon in the Dock to instantly see thumbnails of its open windows. Click to switch, or manage them directly—close, minimize, restore—with precision and speed.

Instant Window Switching

Move your cursor to a Dock icon. Thumbnails appear immediately, letting you jump to the exact window you need without lifting a finger from the trackpad.

Seamless Window Management

Right from the preview panel: close unnecessary windows, minimize to keep your workspace clean, or restore with a single click. No hunting through menus.

Fully Customizable

Tailor themes, thumbnail sizes, animations, and delays to match your workflow. Subtle refinements that feel native to macOS.

Zero System Interference

DockView enhances the Dock without altering it or any system files. Pure, lightweight utility built for reliability.

How It Works

1. Hover to Preview

Position your cursor over the app icon in the Dock. Window thumbnails expand smoothly with customizable animation.

2. Click to Switch

Select any thumbnail to bring that window forward instantly. No app switching or Command-Tab required.

3. Manage on the Fly

Close, minimize, or restore windows directly from the preview. Efficient control at your fingertips.

4. Customize and Go

Adjust sizes, themes, and timing in preferences. It adapts to you, staying out of the way.

Fully. Choose from themes, set thumbnail sizes, enable animations, and fine-tune delays for the perfect feel.
